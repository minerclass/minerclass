# Micah J. Miner, CETL, Ed.S.

Director of Innovation & Technology, Beach Park CCSD 3 (Lake County, IL). Author of *AI Goes to School* (Times 10 Publications). Ed.D. candidate in Curriculum, Advocacy, and Policy at National Louis University. ISTE Community Leader and CoSN committee contributor.

I operate at the practitioner-scholar intersection of K-12 administration, curriculum development, and critical algorithmic research. My work translates complex questions of AI governance, media ecology, and learning science into stable, long-term school strategies.

My scholarship is grounded in more than two decades of public-school experience as a classroom teacher, department chair, principal, technology coach, and central-office administrator. As a CETL-certified district leader, I work with the practical friction of regulatory compliance, data privacy, network security, budgets, and instructional goals.

My dissertation research develops the **Pedagogical Friction Framework**. I study how frictionless algorithmic systems can bypass the productive struggle essential to durable learning, and how educators can decide which noetic, rhetorical, existential, and infrastructural friction to preserve. The repositories below are interactive, public-facing pieces of that research program.

I also believe in open scholarship. Through books, articles, workshops, and public research tools, I translate cognitive science and media ecology into frameworks educators and school leaders can act upon. Reach me at [admin@micahminer.com](mailto:admin@micahminer.com).
