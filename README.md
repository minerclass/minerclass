# Micah J. Miner, CETL, Ed.S.

Director of Innovation & Technology, Beach Park CCSD 3 (Lake County, IL). Author of *AI Goes to School* (Times 10 Publications). Ed.D. candidate in Curriculum, Advocacy, and Policy at National Louis University. ISTE Community Leader; CoSN AI and EdTech Innovation Committees.

My work lives in the space between running district technology infrastructure and critically examining what that technology does to learning. My dissertation research develops the **Pedagogical Friction Framework**: as generative AI removes the productive struggle that durable learning requires, educators need a principled way to decide which friction to preserve. Most of the repos below are interactive, public-facing pieces of that research program.
